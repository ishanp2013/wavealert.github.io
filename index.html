If the ground shakes

Three actions protect you in almost every situation. Practise them before you need them.

  1. Drop

    Get down on your hands and knees right away, so the shaking cannot knock you over.

  2. Cover

    Protect your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk. If there is none, crouch against an inside wall away from windows.

  3. Hold on

    Stay where you are and hold on to your cover until the shaking stops. Do not run outside or use an elevator.

Near the coast? If the shaking is strong or lasts a long time, move to high ground or inland straight away. Do not wait for an official warning.
